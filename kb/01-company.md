  from AI confusion to AI confidence, going department by department to identify high-ROI
  AI opportunities, build workflows and agents, and train teams so the changes stick. `[V:brief]`
- Positioning line used publicly: **AI strategy and implementation for business growth and
  EBITDA improvement**. `[V:snippet]`
