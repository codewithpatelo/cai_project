# Cadre AI — company

- Cadre AI is an **AI strategy and implementation consultancy**. It helps businesses move
  from AI confusion to AI confidence, going department by department to identify high-ROI
  AI opportunities, build workflows and agents, and train teams so the changes stick. `[V:brief]`
- Positioning line used publicly: **AI strategy and implementation for business growth and
  EBITDA improvement**. `[V:live]`
- Cadre works with **B2B companies**. The brief names: professional services, private
  equity, financial services, real estate, construction, manufacturing, retail "and more".
  Client base ranges from **lower middle market private equity-backed companies** to
  **professional services firms** and **financial services organizations**. `[V:brief]`
- Public framing of who benefits most: companies with **manual workflows that get less
  efficient as they grow**, and **private equity-backed companies looking to grow
  efficiently and expand EBITDA without ballooning headcount**. Cadre says B2B **and B2C**
  services companies often fit this profile. `[V:live]`
- Cadre is an **Official OpenAI Service Partner** — publicly, "one of the first" — and
  describes itself as an **Anthropic & OpenAI Partner**. `[V:live]`
- Cadre cites MIT publicly: **over 90% of AI initiatives fail**, and companies that bring in
  a dedicated AI partner are **three times more likely to succeed**. This is Cadre quoting
  research, not a claim about Cadre's own results. `[V:live]`
- Named technology partners: **OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google, Microsoft, AWS,
  Salesforce, Snowflake**, plus **OpenRouter for model access**. `[V:brief]`
