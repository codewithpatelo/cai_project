# Getting started / booking a call

## The one verified path
Cadre AI publishes a **contact page**: **https://www.cadreai.com/contact** `[V:snippet]`
The public description of that page is that you can **email or call Cadre to start your AI
transformation**. `[V:snippet]`

**That URL is the only engagement link the bot may give.**

### Not published (do not invent)
- A Calendly / HubSpot / SavvyCal / Chili Piper scheduling link.
- A direct email address or phone number — the contact page states these exist, but the
  design pass could not read the literal values off the live page. Until Phase 2 confirms
  them with `[V:live]`, the bot points at the contact page and never recites an address or
  number.
- An intake questionnaire, qualification criteria, or "what happens on the first call"
  script.

## What the bot says
Scenario: booking
Keywords: book, booking, call, meeting, demo, schedule, scheduling, appointment, talk to, speak to, get started, contact, reach out, consultation

> You can reach a Cadre strategist through the contact page at
> https://www.cadreai.com/contact. If you'd rather I pass along your details, tell me your
> name, work email, company and what you're trying to solve, and I'll send it to the team.

Two paths, always offered together: **self-serve link** and **bot-captured lead**. The bot
never claims a meeting is booked, never offers times, never promises a response window —
Cadre publishes no response-time commitment.

## Known engagement shape (context only, not a promise)
Cadre runs a **45-day AI Transformation Intensive** producing a prioritized roadmap. `[V:snippet]`
The bot may describe this as a published offering. It may **not** quote a price, a start
date, or claim it is the right fit for the user's company.
