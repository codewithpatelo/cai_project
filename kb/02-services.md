# Services

Cadre AI's four core service lines: **AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation,
AI Engineering, AI Agents**. `[V:brief]`

## AI Strategy
- Transforms a business with a proven AI strategy built on an **8-pillar approach**. `[V:live]`
- Framing: real AI transformation requires **finding the right problems, preparing people
  to lead the change, and implementing solutions that drive measurable results**. `[V:live]`
- Cadre runs a structured **45-day AI Transformation Intensive** that takes a company from
  zero clarity to a prioritized roadmap its own team can execute, finding quick wins with
  measurable EBITDA impact before building and implementing the roadmap. `[V:live]`
  Source page: https://www.cadreai.com/strategy

## AI Leadership & Facilitation
- Moves executives and teams from **AI-hesitant to AI-ambitious**, through facilitation
  backed by **behavioral science** and real-world implementation experience. `[V:live]`
  Source page: https://www.cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation

## AI Engineering
- Builds **workflow automations and AI systems** that integrate disconnected tools and add
  intelligence to repetitive processes, so teams can focus on high-value work. `[V:live]`
- Goal stated publicly: make the tech stack **work as one unified system** — syncing data
  in real time, triggering actions across platforms, eliminating manual copy-paste
  between tools. `[V:live]`
  Source page: https://www.cadreai.com/ai-engineering

## AI Agents
- **Voice agents** handle intake, qualification, support and internal routing, turning
  calls into clean data and next steps. `[V:live]`
- For more complex workflows Cadre builds agents that **plan, take actions across tools,
  and run end-to-end processes with guardrails and human oversight**. `[V:live]`
- Simpler tier: **prompts and assistants** that plug into daily work and automate
  repeatable tasks. `[V:live]`
  Source page: https://www.cadreai.com/agents
