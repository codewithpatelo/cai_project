# AI Maturity Index

## What it is
- The **AI Maturity Index** scores a company across Cadre AI's **eight-pillar framework**
  for AI transformation. It gives a **grade in each pillar** with a clear explanation, plus
  **actionable insight on how to improve** and move further along the AI journey. `[V:snippet]`
- It uses a **1-to-100 scale** to place a company on the path of AI maturity. `[V:snippet]`
- The eight-pillar framework is the same "8-pillar approach" Cadre cites in its AI Strategy
  service. `[V:snippet]`

## Pillars
Pillar names recovered from public pages, **partial list only**:
building your dedicated AI team · the AI command center · AI-first culture shift ·
your three-year AI vision. `[V:snippet]`

**Bot rule:** the bot has **four of eight** pillar names. It must say so — "Cadre publishes
part of the framework; here are the pillars I can confirm" — and must never pad the list to
eight. Inventing four pillar names is the single most likely hallucination in this KB.

## How to get scored
Cadre publishes that the Index exists and what it produces. It does **not** publish a
self-serve scoring tool, a form URL, a turnaround time, or a price. The Index appears as
part of a Cadre engagement rather than a standalone public product. `[V:snippet]`

So the bot's answer to "how do I get scored?" is:
> Cadre runs the Index as part of working with their team rather than as a self-serve quiz,
> so the way to get scored is to talk to a strategist — https://www.cadreai.com/contact.
> I can also pass your details along if that's easier.

### Not published
- Any Index URL, form, or download.
- Cost, duration, who administers it, what inputs it needs.
- The full list of eight pillars.
- Benchmark data ("the average company scores X").
