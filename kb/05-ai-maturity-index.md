# AI Maturity Index

## What it is
- The **AI Maturity Index** scores a company across Cadre AI's **eight-pillar framework**
  for AI transformation. It gives a **grade in each area** with clear explanations, plus
  **actionable insights on how to improve** and move further along the AI journey. `[V:live]`
- The eight-pillar framework is the same "8 Pillars of AI Transformation" Cadre publishes on
  its AI Strategy page. `[V:live]`

## Pillars
All eight are published on https://www.cadreai.com/strategy, in this order: `[V:live]`

1. **Build your dedicated AI team** — a fully accountable leader supported by experts in
   product, strategy, research and engineering, working across departments under CEO
   leadership.
2. **Deploy your AI Command Center** — one company-wide platform (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot,
   Claude) rather than employees using personal accounts with company data.
3. **Create an AI-First Culture Shift** — clear policies, CEO communication that addresses
   employee fears, and real change management.
4. **Connect & Enable your Tech Stack** — API access so agents can move data across
   fragmented platforms, and an audit of tools for AI features already paid for.
5. **AI-Healthy Data Assessment** — clean, structured data, and knowing where data is
   created, transformed and stored.
6. **Build your Framework for AI Agent Readiness** — SOPs, infrastructure access and
   templates, plus ongoing monitoring and feedback.
7. **Departmental AI Deep Dives** — assessing each team's people, processes and technology
   for AI, agentic workflows or traditional automation.
8. **Find your 3-Year AI Vision** — the dream state as the most scalable AI-enabled
   organisation in your category, and a benchmark to measure progress against.

**Bot rule:** the bot may name **all eight**, because Cadre publishes all eight. It should
give them when asked, rather than hedging about an incomplete list.

## How to get scored
Cadre publishes that the Index exists and what it produces. It does **not** publish a
self-serve scoring tool, a form URL, a turnaround time, or a price. The Index appears as
part of a Cadre engagement rather than a standalone public product. `[V:live]`

### Not published
- Any Index URL, form, or download.
- Cost, duration, who administers it, what inputs it needs.
- Any **numeric scoring scale**. Cadre publishes "a grade in each area" and nothing
  numeric; do not describe the Index as scoring out of any number.
- Benchmark data ("the average company scores X").
